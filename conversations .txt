Any asynchronous `fetch()` or `XMLHttpRequest` it performs to pull chapter lists is a **same-origin request**, meaning the browser automatically allows it without CORS restrictions.

  If a site is behind Cloudflare or requires a login, the WebView will load the challenge page. You can display the WebView (or a dialog) to the user once, let them solve the challenge, and then the WebView's session cookies are stored. Any subsequent background JavaScript injection will inherit those authenticated session cookies natively.
* **Minimal Dependencies**:
